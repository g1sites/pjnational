About PJ National

A franchise built on people.

We're a Papa John's franchisee with stores across New York City, New Jersey, and Minnesota. Every shop is run by people who care — about the food, about their teams, and about the neighborhoods they serve.

From our crust to our culture, we believe in doing things the right way. That means quality ingredients, honest work, and creating a place where careers actually grow.
